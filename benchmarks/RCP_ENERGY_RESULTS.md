# RCP Energy Challenge 1 — Executed Results

## Registered workload

The full problem retains 96 fifteen-minute ticks, all 96 cooling choices, all
96 battery choices, storage and thermal state at every tick, grid capacity,
solar supply, source cost, curtailment, and peak grid readout. It does not
collapse the day into one anonymous variable.

The supplied profiles are generated by finite integer rules. They are a
mathematical stress tape, not measurements from a real facility.

Command:

```bash
python3 benchmarks/rcp_energy_challenge.py
python3 validation/rcp_energy_selfcheck.py
```

Raw machine-readable output:
[`rcp_energy_results.json`](rcp_energy_results.json).

## Full 96-tick result

| readout | RCP-Energy | one-tick causal baseline | difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| diesel-output energy | 8.50 kWh-equivalent | 18.75 kWh-equivalent | **−10.25** |
| diesel-output fraction | 34 quanta | 75 quanta | **−41/75 = −54.67%** |
| grid energy | 94.00 kWh | 82.50 kWh | **+11.50** |
| grid peak | 11 kW-readout | 9 kW-readout | +2 |
| total grid + diesel output | 102.50 kWh | 101.25 kWh | **+1.25** |

The correct engineering interpretation is **fuel displacement**, not total
energy reduction. RCP-Energy charges and positions storage before the declared
grid interruption, trading 11.50 kWh of additional grid energy for 10.25
kWh-equivalent less diesel output. Storage loss makes total source energy rise
by 1.25 kWh.

No litres-of-fuel claim is made because the stress tape does not declare a
generator fuel curve.

## Negative controls

The dependency-free self-check requires:

- insufficient candidate budget → `BLOCK`;
- incomplete terminal boundary → `BLOCK`;
- missing witness → `HOLD`;
- different witness → `HOLD`.

These controls prevent a feasible-looking schedule from becoming an
unsupported certificate.

## Tier and honesty fence

Tier: `finite_diagnostic`; general minimality proof: `Th_coqc-elig`.

This result does not show that a real building will save fuel. It shows that,
on the declared finite tape and declared reader priority, RCP-Energy returns a
preserved least-burden history, exposes the energy trade, prices its own
resource consumption before execution, and refuses unsupported evidence.
