The proposition

The finite, stable readout is the real object. The continuum is a name for what you read off it — never a place you have to travel to.

Not the discrete branch of mathematics, and not numerical analysis approximating a continuum believed to be real. We invert which one is primary — and then we prove it, machine-checked, and let you run it.

What it is

Finite & discrete

Computed, not approximated

Every derivative, integral, limit, series, ODE and PDE is a finite readout — finite series, finite differences, finite quadrature. The completed infinity is never formed.

Machine-checked

Axiom-free, or it doesn't count

bash formal/verify.sh compiles 55 Coq theorems and confirms each is Closed under the global context — the keystone, the FTCC bridge, the certified-readout stack, the tropical semirings.

Honest

Certified — or it says HOLD

Tools return (value, proven bound, ACCEPT / HOLD) and refuse to emit a number when their hypotheses fail. Knowing when to say nothing is part of the proof.
